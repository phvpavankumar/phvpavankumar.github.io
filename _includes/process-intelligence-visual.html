AureviaRecording → knowledge

Explore the platform pattern. The separate demo uses fictional content and no live AI.

01 Record a process

The backend stores a recording and creates a tracked job. Upload requests do not wait for AI processing.

02 Generate structured outputs

Workers produce documentation, chapters, guides, diagrams and test cases. Job status and heartbeats keep long-running work visible.

03 Build reusable knowledge

A separate ingest job chunks the generated documentation, creates embeddings and stores searchable knowledge.

04 Retrieve and follow up

The repository combines semantic search with governed SQL and synthesises evidence-backed answers. Conversation history stays scoped to the task.

Team / platform flow above. My contribution focuses on backend integration, orchestration and the knowledge repository.
