## Start with the operational problem

I worked with clients in the United States and Japan to turn inspection ideas into requirements. The work connected what people needed to inspect with how they would use the application, review findings and receive the result.

## Build and validate with the team

I contributed to application design, implementation and debugging, model integration and workflow validation. Working across disciplines helped connect vision processing with the application people actually used. My inspection work also covered image registration, semantic segmentation, RGB-D reconstruction and binocular vision.

## Carry the work through deployment

My involvement extended from the initial requirements through testing and product delivery. The reusable suite brought different inspection tasks into a common application approach. The operational purpose was to give teams organised findings and evidence they could review; no numerical productivity or commercial outcome is claimed here.

## Capabilities by operational need

- **Railway inspection:** track components, signals, vegetation, crossings and paired-camera inspection of moving trains.
- **Infrastructure and energy:** wall cracks, tower defects, solar inspection and aerial asset workflows, including image stitching and bird’s-eye views.
- **Manufacturing quality:** tyre and footwear inspection, comparing candidate defects or part differences for review.
- **Agriculture:** vegetable and pumpkin detection, with item-level inspection workflows.
- **Safety monitoring:** people tracking, platform danger zones and protective-attire review.

## Explore the reconstruction

The fictional **Veyra Vision** demo puts the business problem, inspection example and next operational step first. Optional technical tools reveal annotation, image operations, perspective transforms, manual stitching and delivery planning.

Its prepared detections, replay and release simulation explain how a suite can behave. They are not trained-model outputs or the original implementation. The demo’s additional survey and robotics examples illustrate related concepts rather than extending claims about my responsibilities.
