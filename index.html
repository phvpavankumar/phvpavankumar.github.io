Question 1

What decision are we trying to improve?

Not "where can we use AI" — which actual choice someone makes, and how you would know it got better.

The problem

AI doesn't clear up confusion. It speeds it up.

If two teams already disagree about what "revenue" means, a model will not settle the argument. It will produce that disagreement faster, in more places, with more confidence.

Skip the groundwork

What usually happens

  • The tool gets chosen before the problem is written down
  • Two teams use one word to mean two things
  • Nobody knows which numbers the system may decide alone
  • Six months later, no one can say what it was worth

Do the groundwork

What works instead

  • One named decision, written in a sentence
  • One agreed definition, shared across teams
  • A clear line where a human signs off
  • A baseline agreed up front, so value is provable
